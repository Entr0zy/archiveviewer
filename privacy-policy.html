Archive Viewer Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Archive Viewer is made by Marvin Manu. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Archive Viewer app on iPhone or Android.

The short version

Archive Viewer does not collect, store or share any of your data. There is no account and no sign-up, and the archives you open never leave your phone.

Your archives

When you choose a social-media export (for example an X or Instagram ZIP, or a JSON, CSV, text or HTML file), Archive Viewer copies it into the app's own storage on your device and builds a searchable index there. Posts, captions, dates, pictures, videos and audio are read and shown on the device only. We can't see them, and they are never sent to us or anyone else.

Files and sharing

Archive Viewer only opens files you pick yourself, or files you send to it from another app. "Convert to CSV" creates a file on your device and opens your phone's own share sheet, so the copy goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Links in your posts

Some archived items are only stored as a web link. If you tap "Open reference", the link opens in your browser. Archive Viewer itself makes no network requests with your data.

No tracking

Archive Viewer has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Children

Archive Viewer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use "Forget" in Archive tools to remove the saved archives and index from the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
