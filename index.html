Archive Viewer Support

Something not working, or a question about your archive? Email entrozy@protonmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two days. It helps to say which phone you're on and which service your export came from.

Common questions

Which files can I open? Your downloaded export from X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Mastodon or LinkedIn: the ZIP, or several split ZIP parts chosen together, plus JSON, JavaScript, CSV, TXT and HTML files.

My pictures don't show. Import the complete original ZIP (all parts). Archive Viewer matches pictures, videos and audio to posts from inside the ZIP. Some exports only keep a web link for an item; those show as "reference".

A video won't play on iPhone. iPhone plays MP4 and MOV. For WebM or MKV files, tap "Open in another app".

Where is my data? Only on your phone. There's no account, so nothing is backed up by us. "Forget" in Archive tools, or deleting the app, removes everything it stored.

Can I try it without an archive? Yes. Tap "Try a fictional sample" on the first screen.
